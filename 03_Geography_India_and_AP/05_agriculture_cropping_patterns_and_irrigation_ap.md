# Agriculture & Irrigation in Andhra Pradesh: Cropping Systems & Agrarian Economy


Agriculture forms the foundational backbone of Andhra Pradesh's economy, earning the state the title 'Annapurna' and the 'Rice Bowl of India'. With diverse agro-climatic zones ranging from high-rainfall deltaic plains to dry semi-arid plateaus, Andhra Pradesh leads the nation in paddy, chillies, oil palm, tobacco, and marine aquaculture, supported by major river irrigation networks and mission-mode micro-irrigation.

## 2. Salient Dimensions & Critical Analysis

* Aqua-Hub of India: Andhra Pradesh accounts for over 70% of India's cultured shrimp (Vannamei) exports; coastal belt from Nellore to Srikakulam leads national marine and freshwater fisheries output.

* Handri-Neeva Sujala Sravanthi (HNSS): Longest lift canal project in AP (565 km); lifts flood waters from Srisailam reservoir to provide drinking and irrigation water to drought-prone Rayalaseema districts (Kurnool, Anantapur, Chittoor, Kadapa).

* Galeru-Nagari Sujala Sravanthi (GNSS): Irrigation canal transferring flood waters from the Krishna at Pothireddypadu head regulator through the Veligonda tunnel to the Pennar basin.

* Community Managed Natural Farming (APCNF): Andhra Pradesh leads India's transition to Zero Budget Natural Farming (ZBNF), involving over 700,000 farmers practicing chemical-free regenerative agriculture.

1. Analyze the spatial cropping patterns and agrarian challenges in Andhra Pradesh. How have major lift irrigation projects like HNSS and GNSS helped mitigate regional imbalances between Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema? (APPSC Group-1 Mains, 10 Marks)
-> Model Approach: Contrast the coastal delta agrarian economy (assured canal irrigation, high-yield paddy, aquaculture, commercial tobacco/chilli) with the rainfed, drought-prone Rayalaseema agro-ecology (fragile groundnut mono-cropping, erratic monsoons, falling water tables); analyze the strategic design of mega lift schemes: Handri-Neeva (HNSS) and Galeru-Nagari (GNSS) lifting floodwaters from the Krishna at Srisailam across high ridgelines into Anantapur and Chittoor; evaluate outcomes: stabilised drinking water, revived minor irrigation tanks, and boosted horticulture; discuss unresolved issues: power consumption costs, canal lining, and equitable water distribution.




### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. Which city in Andhra Pradesh houses Asia's largest dedicated market yard for dry red chillies, renowned globally for the GI-tagged 'Guntur Sannam' chilli?
(a) Vijayawada
(b) Guntur
(c) Ongole
(d) Kurnool
Correct Answer: (b) Guntur [Explanation: The Guntur Mirchi Yard is the largest spice market in Asia, trading premium dry chilli varieties exported across the globe.]

* Q2. In which major horticultural plantation crop does Andhra Pradesh rank first in India, accounting for over 85% of total domestic production?
(a) Rubber
(b) Oil Palm
(c) Coffee
(d) Tea
Correct Answer: (b) Oil Palm [Explanation: Andhra Pradesh leads India in oil palm cultivation and palm oil processing, centered primarily in West Godavari and Krishna districts.]

* Q3. The famous 'Banganapalle Mango' (Benishan), which was granted a prestigious Geographical Indication (GI) tag, originates from which district of Andhra Pradesh?
(a) Chittoor
(b) Nandyal / Kurnool
(c) Krishna
(d) Kadapa
Correct Answer: (b) Nandyal / Kurnool [Explanation: Banganapalle mangoes, celebrated for their fiberless pulp and sweet aroma, originate from the Banganapalle princely estate in Kurnool/Nandyal district.]

* Q4. Which district of Andhra Pradesh historically contains the single largest acreage under rain-fed groundnut cultivation in the entire country?
(a) Prakasam
(b) Anantapur
(c) Guntur
(d) Srikakulam
Correct Answer: (b) Anantapur [Explanation: Anantapur district dedicates over 800,000 hectares to rain-fed groundnut cultivation, making it India's premier groundnut basin despite low rainfall.]

* Q5. The Handri-Neeva Sujala Sravanthi (HNSS) project lifts surplus flood waters from which major reservoir to supply irrigation and drinking water to Rayalaseema?
(a) Nagarjuna Sagar Dam
(b) Srisailam Reservoir
(c) Tungabhadra Dam
(d) Somasila Dam
Correct Answer: (b) Srisailam Reservoir [Explanation: HNSS lifts water from the backwaters of Srisailam reservoir at Malyala, channeling it through a 565 km canal network to Kurnool, Anantapur, Kadapa, and Chittoor.]
