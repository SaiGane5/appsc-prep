# Natural Hazards & Disaster Management in AP: Cyclones & Coastal Resilience


Due to its long coastline, low-lying river deltas, and peninsular proximity to the cyclonically active Bay of Bengal, Andhra Pradesh is among India's most hazard-prone states. The state confronts a dual hazard spectrum: recurrent destructive tropical cyclones, storm surges, and riverine floods in coastal districts, alongside persistent chronic droughts in interior Rayalaseema. In response, the Andhra Pradesh State Disaster Management Authority (APSDMA) has pioneered coastal shelters, real-time Doppler radar early warning, and disaster mitigation models.

## 2. Salient Dimensions & Critical Analysis

* APSDMA Institutional Framework: Headed by the Chief Minister; operates the State Emergency Operation Centre (SEOC) in Kunchanapalli, Guntur; collaborates with Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) Doppler Weather Radars (Visakhapatnam and Machilipatnam).

* National Cyclone Risk Mitigation Project (NCRMP): World Bank-assisted project implementing underground electrical cabling in coastal cities, multi-purpose cyclone shelters, saline embankments, and Early Warning Dissemination Systems (EWDS).

* The 1977 Diviseema Cyclone Benchmark: Catastrophic 6-meter storm surge in Krishna district killed 10,000+ people, completely revolutionizing India's disaster management paradigm from reactive relief to proactive disaster preparedness.

* Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ 2019 Notification): Regulates developmental activities within 500 meters of the High Tide Line (HTL) to protect fragile coastal ecosystems and minimize cyclone storm surge damage.

### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. The catastrophic 1977 Diviseema Cyclone, which killed over 10,000 people and revolutionized India's cyclone preparedness paradigm, made landfall in which district?
(a) Visakhapatnam
(b) Krishna (Diviseema)
(c) SPSR Nellore
(d) Srikakulam
Correct Answer: (b) Krishna (Diviseema) [Explanation: The 1977 Andhra cyclone generated a 6-meter tidal wave that engulfed Diviseema in Krishna district on 19 November 1977, driving the modern cyclone shelter program.]

* Q2. The State Emergency Operation Centre (SEOC) of the Andhra Pradesh State Disaster Management Authority (APSDMA) is headquartered at:
(a) Visakhapatnam
(b) Kunchanapalli (Guntur district)
(c) Tirupati
(d) Kurnool
Correct Answer: (b) Kunchanapalli (Guntur district) [Explanation: The state-of-the-art emergency operations center of APSDMA is situated at Kunchanapalli near Tadepalli in Guntur district.]

* Q3. Coastal village Uppada in Kakinada district is renowned in disaster engineering for implementing which innovative coastal protection measure against severe sea erosion?
(a) Concrete Groynes
(b) Geotextile Tube (Geo-tube) Seawall
(c) Breakwater Atoll
(d) Mangrove Lagoon Channel
Correct Answer: (b) Geotextile Tube (Geo-tube) Seawall [Explanation: Uppada implemented India's pioneering Geo-tube synthetic textile revetment to arrest rapid coastal erosion caused by high wave energy.]

* Q4. Under Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ) regulations in India, how many meters from the High Tide Line (HTL) on the landward side are legally designated as a regulated coastal buffer zone?
(a) 100 meters
(b) 200 meters
(c) 500 meters
(d) 1000 meters
Correct Answer: (c) 500 meters [Explanation: The CRZ notification demarcates coastal stretches within 500 meters of the High Tide Line (HTL) as strictly regulated zones to conserve marine ecology.]

* Q5. Which community-based volunteer training programme is implemented by the National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) and APSDMA to train local youth in emergency flood and cyclone rescue?
(a) Aapda Mitra Scheme
(b) Bharat Rakshak
(c) Sagar Kavach
(d) Jal Suraksha Doot
Correct Answer: (a) Aapda Mitra Scheme [Explanation: The 'Aapda Mitra' scheme trains grassroots community volunteers in disaster-prone districts with basic search, rescue, and first-aid skills to serve as first responders.]
