# Biodiversity, Forests & Protected Areas of AP: Wetlands & Biosphere Reserves


Andhra Pradesh encompasses critical ecological habitats within the Eastern Ghats and the Coromandel coast. The state is home to India's largest tiger reserve (Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam), the Seshachalam Biosphere Reserve, national parks at Papikonda and Sri Venkateswara, the coastal wetland of Kolleru Lake (Ramsar Site), and the Pulicat brackish lagoon, which host rare endemic wildlife such as the golden gecko, slender loris, and Jerdon's courser.

## 2. Salient Dimensions & Critical Analysis

* Jerdon's Courser (Rhinoptilus bitorquatus): Nocturnal cursorial bird thought extinct until rediscovered in 1986 in the Sri Lankamalleswara Wildlife Sanctuary (Kadapa district); critically endangered on the IUCN Red List.

* Golden Gecko (Calodactylodes aureus): Rare rock-dwelling endemic lizard found exclusively in the deep rocky ravines of the Seshachalam and Tirumala hills.

* Operation Kolleru (2006): Massive environmental restoration drive executed by the Supreme Court of India and AP Forest Department, demolishing illegal commercial aquaculture bunds across 15,000 acres to restore the natural hydrological flow of the Ramsar wetland.

* Mangrove Ecosystem at Coringa: Ramsar site candidate; supports 35 mangrove plant species (Avicennia, Rhizophora) and a thriving population of the rare fishing cat (Prionailurus viverrinus).

### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. Which is the largest Tiger Reserve in India by total surface area, spanning the Nallamala hill forests across Andhra Pradesh?
(a) Jim Corbett Tiger Reserve
(b) Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam Tiger Reserve (NSTR)
(c) Bandipur Tiger Reserve
(d) Simlipal Tiger Reserve
Correct Answer: (b) Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam Tiger Reserve (NSTR) [Explanation: The Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam Tiger Reserve, covering over 3,700 sq km in the Nallamala range, is the largest tiger reserve in India.]

* Q2. The critically endangered nocturnal bird 'Jerdon's Courser' was dramatically rediscovered in 1986 in which wildlife sanctuary of Andhra Pradesh?
(a) Coringa Wildlife Sanctuary
(b) Sri Lankamalleswara Wildlife Sanctuary
(c) Rollapadu Bird Sanctuary
(d) Kaundinya Sanctuary
Correct Answer: (b) Sri Lankamalleswara Wildlife Sanctuary [Explanation: Believed extinct for over 80 years, Jerdon's Courser was rediscovered in 1986 in Sri Lankamalleswara Wildlife Sanctuary in Kadapa.]

* Q3. Which freshwater lake in Andhra Pradesh was designated as a Ramsar Wetland of International Importance in 2002?
(a) Pulicat Lake
(b) Kolleru Lake
(c) Kondakarla Ava
(d) Kanwar Lake
Correct Answer: (b) Kolleru Lake [Explanation: Kolleru Lake was declared a Ramsar wetland in November 2002, functioning as a major breeding habitat for the Spot-billed Pelican and Painted Stork.]

* Q4. Sullurpeta in Tirupati (Nellore) district is famous for hosting which annual eco-tourism festival celebrating the arrival of thousands of migratory birds at Pulicat Lake?
(a) Pelican Festival
(b) Flamingo Festival
(c) Hornbill Festival
(d) Olive Ridley Festival
Correct Answer: (b) Flamingo Festival [Explanation: The annual Flamingo Festival is held at Sullurpeta and Nelapattu to celebrate the massive migration of Greater Flamingos to Pulicat Lagoon.]

* Q5. Rollapadu Wildlife Sanctuary in Nandyal/Kurnool district is dedicated primarily to the conservation of which critically endangered grassland bird?
(a) Great Indian Bustard
(b) Jerdon's Courser
(c) Bengal Florican
(d) Lesser Florican
Correct Answer: (a) Great Indian Bustard [Explanation: Rollapadu Wildlife Sanctuary in the semi-arid grasslands of Kurnool was established to protect the critically endangered Great Indian Bustard (Ardeotis nigriceps).]
