# Demographic Profile & Tribal Communities of Andhra Pradesh


The demographic profile of Andhra Pradesh reflects advanced stages of the demographic transition, characterized by a declining total fertility rate (TFR below replacement level at 1.5), an aging population structure, and expanding urbanization. The state also harbors rich indigenous anthropological heritage, being home to 33 Scheduled Tribe communities including seven Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs) inhabiting the Eastern Ghats and Nallamala forests.

## 2. Salient Dimensions & Critical Analysis

* Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs) in AP: 7 designated PVTGs: Chenchus (Nallamalas), Konda Reddis (Godavari gorge/Papikondas), Kolams, Porjas, Khonds, Gadabas, and Savaras (Srikakulam); characterized by pre-agricultural technology, stagnant population, and low literacy.

* The Chenchu Community: Hunter-gatherer semi-nomadic tribe dwelling in the Nallamala forest tiger reserve (Nagarjunasagar-Srisailam); traditional gatherers of wild honey, gum, and medicinal herbs; speak Chenchu (Dravidian tongue).

* Yanadi Tribe: Inhabiting the coastal plains of Nellore, Bapatla, and Chittoor; traditional expertise in rodent catch, snake tracking, and fishing; historically linked to the Sriharikota missile launch station (SHAR) rehabilitation.

* Integrated Tribal Development Agencies (ITDAs): Functioning in Paderu (ASR district), Rampachodavaram, Seethampeta, K.R. Puram, and Srisailam (Chenchu ITDA) to safeguard tribal land rights under the 1/70 Land Transfer Regulation and PESA Act.

### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. According to the 2011 Census, what is the overall sex ratio (females per 1,000 males) for the state of Andhra Pradesh?
(a) 943
(b) 974
(c) 997
(d) 1004
Correct Answer: (c) 997 [Explanation: Andhra Pradesh recorded a healthy sex ratio of 997 females per 1,000 males in Census 2011, notably above the national average of 943.]

* Q2. Which indigenous Scheduled Tribe of Andhra Pradesh, classified as a PVTG, is traditionally forest-dwelling in the Nallamala hills and practices honey collection?
(a) Lambada
(b) Chenchu
(c) Yerukula
(d) Valmiki
Correct Answer: (b) Chenchu [Explanation: The Chenchus are an ancient hunter-gatherer Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Group inhabiting the Nallamala forests across Kurnool, Nalgonda, and Prakasam.]

* Q3. How many tribal communities in Andhra Pradesh are officially designated by the Government of India as Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs)?
(a) 4
(b) 7
(c) 12
(d) 15
Correct Answer: (b) 7 [Explanation: Andhra Pradesh has 7 designated PVTGs: Chenchus, Konda Reddis, Kolams, Khonds, Porjas, Savaras, and Gadabas.]

* Q4. Which landmark state regulation strictly prohibits the transfer of any land in Scheduled Agency tracts of Andhra Pradesh to non-tribal individuals?
(a) Land Ceiling Act 1973
(b) AP Agency Land Transfer Regulation 1 of 1970 (LTR 1/70)
(c) PESA Act 1996
(d) Forest Conservation Act
Correct Answer: (b) AP Agency Land Transfer Regulation 1 of 1970 (LTR 1/70) [Explanation: Regulation 1 of 1970 (amending 1959 regulations) created an absolute statutory presumption that all land in Agency tracts belongs to tribals, barring transfer to non-tribals.]

* Q5. Which district in residual Andhra Pradesh holds the highest population density per square kilometer according to the 2011 Census?
(a) Visakhapatnam
(b) Krishna
(c) Guntur
(d) East Godavari
Correct Answer: (b) Krishna [Explanation: In the 2011 Census, Krishna district recorded the highest population density in the state at 518 persons per sq km, driven by fertile deltaic agriculture and urban centers.]
