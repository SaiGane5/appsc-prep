# Mineral & Energy Resources of India & AP: Barytes, Bauxite, Uranium & KG Basin


Andhra Pradesh is recognized as the 'Mineral Storehouse of South India', possessing vast non-metallic and strategic mineral reserves. The state holds 97% of India's barytes (Mangampeta in Kadapa), world-class bauxite deposits in the Eastern Ghats, extensive cement-grade limestone across the Palnadu and Kurnool formations, the Tummalapalle uranium reserves, and the deepwater offshore hydrocarbon fields of the Krishna-Godavari (KG) Basin.

## 1. Institutional & Structural Framework


### Table: Key Mineral & Energy Reserves in Andhra Pradesh

| Mineral / Resource | Key Geological Locations in AP | Reserve Status & National Share | Industrial & Strategic Applications |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Barytes (Barium Sulphate) | Mangampeta, YSR Kadapa district (Cuddapah Basin). | Contains 97% of India's and 28% of the world's bedded barytes reserves. | Crucial weighting agent in deep oil and gas drilling muds; barium chemical industries. |
| Bauxite (Aluminium Ore) | Eastern Ghats belt: Araku, Chintapalli, Ananthagiri (ASR & Visakhapatnam districts). | Second largest bauxite reserves in India (over 600 million tonnes of gibbsitic ore). | Raw material for alumina refineries and aluminium smelting. |
| Limestone (Cement Grade) | Palnadu (Guntur), Kurnool, YSR Kadapa, Anantapur. | One of India's leading producers; vast Narji limestone beds. | Major cement production hub of South India (cement capital at Macherla/Tadipatri). |
| Uranium (Nuclear Fuel) | Tummalapalle, Vemula mandal, YSR Kadapa district. | One of the largest uranium reserves in the world (estimated 150,000+ tonnes of U3O8). | Domestic fuel feed for India's nuclear power reactors (NPCIL). |
| Heavy Mineral Beach Sands | Bhavanapadu (Srikakulam), Bheemunipatnam (Vizag), Nizampatnam. | Rich in Monazite (thorium source), Ilmenite, Rutile, Zircon, Garnet. | Atomic minerals, titanium pigment, aerospace alloys, coastal sand mining. |
| Natural Gas & Crude Oil | Krishna-Godavari (KG) Offshore Basin (KG-D6, Ravva field). | Deepwater offshore sedimentary basin; major discoveries by ONGC and Reliance. | Power generation, fertilizer production, City Gas Distribution, and national energy security. |



## 2. Salient Dimensions & Critical Analysis

* Mangampeta Barytes Deposit: Unique volcanogenic bedded deposit discovered by Geological Survey of India; mined by Andhra Pradesh Mineral Development Corporation (APMDC); exported globally to Gulf oil fields.

* Tummalapalle Uranium Mine: Operated by Uranium Corporation of India Limited (UCIL); hosted in dolostone rocks of the Vempalle formation; low to medium-grade ore accessible through underground decline mining.

* Renewable Energy Leadership: Kurnool Ultra Mega Solar Park (1,000 MW, one of the world's pioneering single-location solar parks); Ananthapuramu Solar Park (1,500 MW); pumped storage hydro projects (Pinnapuram Integrated Renewable Energy Project).

* Thermal Power Hubs: Dr. Narla Tata Rao Thermal Power Station (VTPS, Ibrahimpatnam), Sri Damodaram Sanjeevaiah Thermal Power Station (Krishnapatnam), NTPC Simhadri (Visakhapatnam).

### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. The world's largest single deposit of bedded barytes, accounting for over 97% of India's total reserves, is located at which site in Andhra Pradesh?
(a) Tummalapalle
(b) Mangampeta
(c) Bheemunipatnam
(d) Macherla
Correct Answer: (b) Mangampeta [Explanation: Mangampeta in YSR Kadapa district possesses the world's largest known bedded barytes deposit, mined open-cast by the APMDC.]

* Q2. The massive domestic uranium deposit discovered by the Atomic Minerals Directorate and mined by UCIL is situated at Tummalapalle in which district of AP?
(a) Kurnool
(b) YSR Kadapa
(c) Anantapur
(d) Chittoor
Correct Answer: (b) YSR Kadapa [Explanation: Tummalapalle in YSR Kadapa district holds one of the largest uranium reserves in the world, with confirmed deposits exceeding 150,000 tonnes.]

* Q3. Which major deepwater offshore sedimentary basin in coastal Andhra Pradesh holds vast natural gas reserves, including the historic KG-D6 gas field?
(a) Cauvery Basin
(b) Krishna-Godavari (KG) Basin
(c) Mahanadi Basin
(d) Cambay Basin
Correct Answer: (b) Krishna-Godavari (KG) Basin [Explanation: The Krishna-Godavari (KG) offshore basin holds India's premier deepwater natural gas and crude reserves, operated by ONGC and Reliance.]

* Q4. Heavy mineral beach sands along the coastal belt of Srikakulam and Visakhapatnam contain rich concentrations of 'Monazite', which is the primary source of:
(a) Uranium
(b) Thorium
(c) Titanium
(d) Beryllium
Correct Answer: (b) Thorium [Explanation: Monazite beach sand contains rich concentrations of Thorium and rare earth elements, vital for the third stage of India's nuclear power programme.]

* Q5. The 1,000 MW Kurnool Ultra Mega Solar Park, one of the earliest mega single-location solar power installations in India, is situated in which mandal?
(a) Orvakal
(b) Bethamcherla
(c) Banaganapalle
(d) Dhone
Correct Answer: (a) Orvakal [Explanation: The 1,000 MW Kurnool Solar Park is situated across Orvakal mandal in Kurnool district, commissioned over 5,900 acres.]
