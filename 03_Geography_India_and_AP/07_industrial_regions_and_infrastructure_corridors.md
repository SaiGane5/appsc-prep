# Industrial Regions & Corridors of AP: VCIC, CBIC & Major Ports


Andhra Pradesh occupies a prime geostrategic gateway position on India's eastern seaboard, serving as the multimodal logistics hub connecting the hinterland to Southeast Asia. Driven by two national industrial economic corridors—the Visakhapatnam-Chennai Industrial Corridor (VCIC) and the Chennai-Bengaluru Industrial Corridor (CBIC)—and a network of deep-water ports (Visakhapatnam, Krishnapatnam, Gangavaram, Ramayapatnam), the state is accelerating port-led industrialization.

## 2. Salient Dimensions & Critical Analysis

* Jawaharlal Nehru Pharma City (JNPC, Parawada): India's premier bulk drug and pharmaceutical manufacturing park in Visakhapatnam, housing global giants like Pfizer, Mylan, and Reddy's.

* Sri City Special Economic Zone: Located on the AP-Tamil Nadu border in Tirupati district; integrated multi-product industrial hub housing 200+ global manufacturing MNCs (Alstom metro coaches, Isuzu, Foxconn, Daikin, Mondelez).

* Visakhapatnam Steel Plant (Rashtriya Ispat Nigam Limited - RINL): India's first shore-based integrated steel plant; operates 7.3 MTPA liquid steel capacity.

* Automobile Clusters: Anantapur automotive manufacturing cluster anchored by Kia Motors India (Erandahalli plant producing Seltos and Sonet).

### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. Which multilateral financial development institution is the primary funding partner for the Visakhapatnam-Chennai Industrial Corridor (VCIC)?
(a) World Bank
(b) Asian Development Bank (ADB)
(c) New Development Bank (NDB)
(d) Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB)
Correct Answer: (b) Asian Development Bank (ADB) [Explanation: The Asian Development Bank (ADB) is the lead partner financing the VCIC, which is the first phase of the East Coast Economic Corridor (ECEC).]

* Q2. The world-class integrated multi-product industrial city and Special Economic Zone 'Sri City' is strategically located in which district of Andhra Pradesh?
(a) Visakhapatnam
(b) Tirupati (Sathyavedu)
(c) Guntur
(d) Kurnool
Correct Answer: (b) Tirupati (Sathyavedu) [Explanation: Sri City is situated in Sathyavedu constituency of Tirupati district on NH-16, adjoining the Tamil Nadu border, housing global manufacturing enterprises.]

* Q3. India's first shore-based integrated steel manufacturing plant, Rashtriya Ispat Nigam Limited (RINL), is situated at:
(a) Machilipatnam
(b) Visakhapatnam
(c) Kakinada
(d) Krishnapatnam
Correct Answer: (b) Visakhapatnam [Explanation: Visakhapatnam Steel Plant (Vizag Steel / RINL) was commissioned in 1990 as India's first coastal shore-based integrated steel plant.]

* Q4. Which private deepwater port in Andhra Pradesh, developed on the coast of SPSR Nellore district, is among the fastest-growing modern container and bulk cargo ports?
(a) Kakinada Port
(b) Krishnapatnam Port
(c) Bhavanapadu Port
(d) Nizampatnam Port
Correct Answer: (b) Krishnapatnam Port [Explanation: Krishnapatnam Port (operated by Adani Ports) in Nellore is a major deepwater, all-weather automated commercial port on the east coast.]

* Q5. The multinational automobile manufacturing facility of Kia Motors India is established at Erandahalli in which district of Andhra Pradesh?
(a) Chittoor
(b) Anantapur (Sri Sathya Sai)
(c) YSR Kadapa
(d) Kurnool
Correct Answer: (b) Anantapur (Sri Sathya Sai) [Explanation: Kia Motors set up its $1.1 billion integrated car manufacturing facility at Penukonda/Erandahalli in Sri Sathya Sai (Anantapur) district in 2017.]
