# Freedom Movement in Andhra: Vandemataram to Salt Satyagraha


The nationalist freedom struggle found vibrant, militant expression across Andhra. Ignited by Bipin Chandra Pal's tour of coastal Andhra in 1907 during the Vandemataram Movement, the region mounted heroic mass struggles during the Non-Cooperation movement—most notably the historic Chirala-Perala municipal exodus, the Palnadu Forest Satyagraha, and the Pedanandipadu No-Tax campaign—culminating in the defiance of Tanguturi Prakasam Pantulu ('Andhra Kesari') during the Salt Satyagraha.

| Pedanandipadu No-Tax Campaign (1922) | Pedanandipadu firka, Bapatla; Parvataneni Veeraiah Chaudhari ('Andhra Shivaji'). | Total civil disobedience: complete non-payment of land revenue; village officers (Karnams and Munsiffs) resigned en masse; complete boycott of British officials. | Revenue administration collapsed entirely in the firka; Gandhi advised pausing until nation was ready, preventing immediate military slaughter. |

## 2. Salient Dimensions & Critical Analysis

* Tanguturi Prakasam Pantulu ('Andhra Kesari'): Bare-chested courage during Simon Commission protests in Madras on 3 Feb 1928 when police opened fire; dared officers to shoot him; later became Premier of Madras Presidency (1946) and the First Chief Minister of Andhra State (1953).

* Mutnuri Krishna Rao & 'Krishna Patrika': Founded in 1902 in Machilipatnam; fearless Telugu nationalist weekly newspaper that nurtured generations of freedom fighters and intellectual nationalists.

* Kasinadhuni Nageswara Rao: Founder of 'Amrutanjan' and the influential Telugu daily 'Andhra Patrika' (1908 in Bombay, shifted to Madras in 1914); established 'Andhra Grantha Mandali'; acclaimed as 'Desoddharaka'.

* Durgabai Deshmukh: Organized Salt Satyagraha in Madras city along with Prakasam; imprisoned for one year at Vellore; later founded the Andhra Mahila Sabha in Madras (1937) and served on the Planning Commission.

### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. Who organized the historic 11-month mass evacuation of Chirala-Perala in 1921, establishing the parallel township 'Ramanagar' with his volunteer corps 'Ramadandu'?
(a) Parvataneni Veeraiah Chaudhari
(b) Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya ('Andhra Ratna')
(c) Konda Venkatappaiah
(d) Tanguturi Prakasam
Correct Answer: (b) Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya ('Andhra Ratna') [Explanation: Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya led 15,000 residents out of Chirala to live in thatched huts at 'Ramanagar' rather than pay municipal taxes.]

* Q2. Which fearless nationalist leader earned the title 'Andhra Kesari' (Lion of Andhra) after baring his chest to armed British police bayonets during the Simon Commission boycott in Madras?
(a) Tanguturi Prakasam Pantulu
(b) Potti Sreeramulu
(c) Gadicherla Harisarvothama Rao
(d) Kasinadhuni Nageswara Rao
Correct Answer: (a) Tanguturi Prakasam Pantulu [Explanation: On 3 February 1928, Tanguturi Prakasam bared his chest before armed British soldiers in Madras, declaring: 'Shoot if you have the courage!', earning the title 'Andhra Kesari'.]

* Q3. Who led the heroic Palnadu Forest Satyagraha in Guntur district in 1921 against oppressive British forest grazing taxes (Pullari), attaining martyrdom in police firing at Minchalapadu?
(a) Alluri Sitarama Raju
(b) Kannuganti Hanumanthu
(c) Parvataneni Veeraiah Chaudhari
(d) Konda Venkatappaiah
Correct Answer: (b) Kannuganti Hanumanthu [Explanation: Kannuganti Hanumanthu led the Palnadu cattle-grazing tax resistance and was martyred when British police opened fire on his peasant gathering at Minchalapadu on 26 February 1922.]

* Q4. Which charismatic peasant leader, popularly acclaimed as 'Andhra Shivaji', organized the celebrated Pedanandipadu No-Tax campaign in 1922 that completely paralyzed British revenue collection?
(a) Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya
(b) Parvataneni Veeraiah Chaudhari
(c) Konda Venkatappaiah
(d) Bikkina Venkataratnam
Correct Answer: (b) Parvataneni Veeraiah Chaudhari [Explanation: Parvataneni Veeraiah Chaudhari organized the Pedanandipadu No-Tax campaign where all village revenue officers resigned, earning him the title 'Andhra Shivaji'.]

* Q5. Which historic nationalist Telugu daily newspaper was founded by Kasinadhuni Nageswara Rao in 1908 to advance the cause of Indian independence and Andhra culture?
(a) Krishna Patrika
(b) Andhra Patrika
(c) Vivekavardhini
(d) Desabhimani
Correct Answer: (b) Andhra Patrika [Explanation: 'Desoddharaka' Kasinadhuni Nageswara Rao founded 'Andhra Patrika' in Bombay in 1908 (shifted to Madras in 1914), serving as the paramount voice of the Telugu nationalist movement.]
