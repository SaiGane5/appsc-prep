# Social Reform Movement in Andhra: Veeresalingam, Gurazada & Gidugu


The late 19th and early 20th centuries witnessed the glorious flowering of the 'Andhra Cultural Renaissance'. Spearheaded by Kandukuri Veeresalingam Pantulu ('Gadya Tikkana'), the movement crusaded against child marriage, female illiteracy, and enforced widowhood. Simultaneously, Mahakavi Gurazada Apparao revolutionized drama with 'Kanyasulkam', Gidugu Ramamurthy democratized literature through the Spoken Telugu Movement (Vyavaharika Bhasha), and Sir Raghupathi Venkataratnam Naidu pioneered Brahmo social justice and anti-devadasi reforms.

## 2. Salient Dimensions & Critical Analysis

* First Widow Remarriage (11 December 1881, Rajahmundry): Veeresalingam solemnized the marriage of Gogulapati Sreeramulu and Rajalaxmi amid fierce orthodox opposition, guarded by police and student volunteers.

* Kanyasulkam (1892): Masterpiece play by Gurazada featuring the unforgettable characters Girisam (pseudo-modern fraud), Madhuravani (noble reformed courtesan), Ramappapanthulu, and Lubdhavadhanulu; celebrated for its razor-sharp colloquial Telugu dialogue.

* Komarraju Lakshmana Rao (1877–1923): Founded the 'Vignana Chandrika Mandali' in 1906 to publish modern science and historical books in Telugu; pioneered the Telugu encyclopaedia project ('Andhra Vijnana Sarvasvamu').

* Library Movement in Andhra: Started with the Krishna Deva Raya Andhra Bhasha Nilayam (Hyderabad 1901) and Saraswata Niketanam (Vetapalem); popularized by Gadicherla Harisarvothama Rao and Ayyanki Venkata Ramanayya.

-> Model Approach: Define the socio-cultural decadence of late 19th-century Andhra (enforced widowhood, child marriage, kanyasulkam, koota-sastra hypocrisy); evaluate Veeresalingam's heroic crusade: publishing 'Vivekavardhini', founding Hitakarini Samajam, solemnizing the historic first widow remarriage (1881), and pioneering modern Telugu prose genres (first novel, first autobiography); examine Gurazada Apparao's literary-social revolution: using living spoken Telugu in 'Kanyasulkam' (1892) to expose bride-price and conservative greed; analyze his secular patriotic poetry ('Desamunu Preminchumanna') defining nation as living people rather than soil; conclude on how their twin crusades birthed modern progressive Andhra society.




### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. Which pioneer of the Andhra Renaissance solemnized the historic First Widow Remarriage in Andhra on 11 December 1881 at Rajahmundry?
(a) Gurazada Apparao
(b) Kandukuri Veeresalingam Pantulu
(c) Raghupathi Venkataratnam Naidu
(d) Gidugu Ramamurthy
Correct Answer: (b) Kandukuri Veeresalingam Pantulu [Explanation: Kandukuri Veeresalingam founded the Rajahmundry Social Reform Association and conducted the first widow remarriage on 11 December 1881.]

* Q2. The landmark modern Telugu satirical social drama 'Kanyasulkam', targeting the abhorrent practice of bride-price and child marriage, was authored in 1892 by:
(a) Kandukuri Veeresalingam
(b) Gurazada Venkata Apparao
(c) Chilakamarti Lakshmi Narasimham
(d) Panuganti Lakshminarasimha Rao
Correct Answer: (b) Gurazada Venkata Apparao [Explanation: Mahakavi Gurazada Apparao wrote 'Kanyasulkam' (1892) in colloquial spoken Telugu, immortalizing iconic characters like Girisam and Madhuravani.]

* Q3. The birthday of which visionary scholar, who spearheaded the 'Vyavaharika Bhasha' (Living Spoken Telugu) movement, is celebrated annually on 29 August as 'Telugu Language Day'?
(a) Nannaya Bhatta
(b) Gidugu Venkata Ramamurthy
(c) C.P. Brown
(d) Komarraju Lakshmana Rao
Correct Answer: (b) Gidugu Venkata Ramamurthy [Explanation: 29 August is celebrated across Andhra Pradesh as 'Telugu Bhasha Dinotsavam' in honour of Gidugu Ramamurthy's pioneering crusade for spoken Telugu.]

* Q4. Which was the very first full-length original novel published in the Telugu language, authored by Kandukuri Veeresalingam in 1880?
(a) Kanyasulkam
(b) Rajasekhara Charitramu
(c) Malapalli
(d) Chivaraku Migiledi
Correct Answer: (b) Rajasekhara Charitramu [Explanation: 'Rajasekhara Charitramu' (1880), modeled partly on Goldsmith's 'Vicar of Wakefield', was the first full-length novel written in the Telugu language by Veeresalingam.]

* Q5. Which eminent educationist and Brahmo leader, acclaimed as 'Brahmarshi', led the crusade against the Devadasi system and nautch parties in coastal Andhra?
(a) Raghupathi Venkataratnam Naidu
(b) Kasinadhuni Nageswara Rao
(c) Mutnuri Krishna Rao
(d) Tanguturi Prakasam
Correct Answer: (a) Raghupathi Venkataratnam Naidu [Explanation: Sir Raghupathi Venkataratnam Naidu founded the Social Purity Association and spearheaded the anti-devadasi and anti-nautch movement across the Madras Presidency.]
