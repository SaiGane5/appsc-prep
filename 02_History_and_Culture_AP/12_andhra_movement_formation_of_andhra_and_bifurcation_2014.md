# Andhra Movement, Linguistic Statehood (1953/1956) & Bifurcation 2014


The modern political identity of Andhra Pradesh was forged through a century of struggles: the Andhra Movement initiated at the Bapatla Mahasabha (1913), the harmonious Sri Bagh Pact (1937), the supreme martyrdom of Amarajeevi Potti Sreeramulu resulting in the creation of Andhra State at Kurnool (1953), and the integration with Telangana under the Gentlemen's Agreement to form Andhra Pradesh (1956). In 2014, the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act divided the state, creating Telangana and confronting residual Andhra Pradesh with deep fiscal, institutional, and capital city challenges.

## 2. Salient Dimensions & Critical Analysis

* Amarajeevi Potti Sreeramulu: Disciple of Mahatma Gandhi; commenced fast at Bulusu Sambamurti's house in Madras on 19 October 1952; died on 15 December 1952; sacrifice compelled Nehru to concede Andhra State four days later.

* Kurnool Capital Era (1953–1956): Tent offices and legislative assembly functioned in Kurnool; historic compromise under the Sri Bagh Pact.

* Telangana Agitations: 1969 Jai Telangana movement (Marri Channa Reddy) and 1972 Jai Andhra movement led to the Six-Point Formula (1973) and 32nd Constitutional Amendment (Article 371D).

* Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act 2014 Key Sections: Section 46 (special fiscal grants for Rayalaseema and North Coastal Andhra), Section 90 (Polavaram Irrigation Project as National Project funded 100% by Union government), Section 94 (tax incentives for industrial development), and Section 93 (creation of new capital, railway zone in Vizag, national educational institutes).

### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. Where was the historic First Andhra Mahasabha held in May 1913 under the presidency of Bayya Narasimheswara Sarma, officially launching the Andhra Movement?
(a) Vijayawada
(b) Bapatla
(c) Rajahmundry
(d) Guntur
Correct Answer: (b) Bapatla [Explanation: The First Andhra Conference was held at Bapatla in Guntur district on 26–27 May 1913, formally giving birth to the organized Andhra movement.]

* Q2. The historic 'Sri Bagh Pact', signed on 14 November 1937 between leaders of Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema to foster regional trust, was concluded at the residence of:
(a) Tanguturi Prakasam
(b) Kasinadhuni Nageswara Rao
(c) Konda Venkatappaiah
(d) Kalluri Subba Rao
Correct Answer: (b) Kasinadhuni Nageswara Rao [Explanation: The pact was signed at 'Sri Bagh', the Madras residence of Desoddharaka Kasinadhuni Nageswara Rao, guaranteeing university and capital concessions to Rayalaseema.]

* Q3. Under the historic 'Gentlemen's Agreement' signed in Delhi in February 1956, how many leaders from Andhra and Telangana respectively participated in formulating the safeguards?
(a) 2 from each region
(b) 4 from each region (total 8)
(c) 6 from each region
(d) 10 from each region
Correct Answer: (b) 4 from each region (total 8) [Explanation: Eight leaders (four from Andhra: Gopala Reddy, Sanjeeva Reddy, Latchanna, Satyanarayana Raju; and four from Telangana: Burgula, Ranga Reddy, Channa Reddy, Narsing Rao) signed the agreement.]

* Q4. Which multipurpose irrigation project on the Godavari River was declared as a 'National Project' by the Central Government under Section 90 of the AP Reorganisation Act 2014?
(a) Nagarjuna Sagar Project
(b) Polavaram Irrigation Project
(c) Srisailam Project
(d) Veligonda Project
Correct Answer: (b) Polavaram Irrigation Project [Explanation: Section 90 of the AP Reorganisation Act 2014 declared the Polavaram Multipurpose National Irrigation Project to be executed and financed by the Union government.]

* Q5. Who was sworn in as the first Chief Minister of the newly formed Andhra Pradesh state on 1 November 1956 with Hyderabad as its capital?
(a) Tanguturi Prakasam Pantulu
(b) Bezawada Gopala Reddy
(c) Neelam Sanjeeva Reddy
(d) Burgula Ramakrishna Rao
Correct Answer: (c) Neelam Sanjeeva Reddy [Explanation: Neelam Sanjeeva Reddy became the first Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh on 1 November 1956, while Burgula Ramakrishna Rao was the last CM of Hyderabad State.]
