# Qutub Shahis of Golconda: Administration, Telugu Literature & Kuchipudi


The Qutub Shahi Sultanate of Golconda (1518–1687 CE), founded by Sultan Quli Qutb Shah, fostered an extraordinary era of composite Telugu-Persian cultural synthesis across Andhra and Telangana. Renowned for their secular administrative appointments (Madanna and Akkanna), royal patronage of Telugu literature under Ibrahim Qutb Shah ('Malkibharama'), the musical padas of Kancharla Gopanna (Bhakta Ramadasu) at Bhadrachalam, and the classical codification of Kuchipudi dance by Siddhendra Yogi, Golconda represented the peak of Deccan syncretism.

## 1. Institutional & Structural Framework


### Table: Cultural & Administrative Spectrum of the Golconda Sultanate

| Monarch & Reign | Administrative Milestone & Appointments | Telugu Literary Patronage | Cultural & Architectural Legacy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sultan Quli Qutb Shah (1518–1543 CE) | Declared independence from Bahmani Sultanate; fortified Golconda. | Encouraged local Telugu officers and revenue collectors. | Excavated Golconda hill citadel; introduced Perso-Arabic administrative vocabulary into Telugu. |
| Ibrahim Qutb Shah (1550–1580 CE) | Long exile at Vijayanagara (1543–1550) gave him deep love for Telugu; allied with Deccan sultans at Talikota (1565). | Warmly called 'Malkibharama' by Telugu poets; court poet Addanki Gangadhara Kavi dedicated 'Tapati Samvaranopadhyanamu'; patronized Ponnikanti Telaganarya who composed 'Yayati Charitra' (first Accha Telugu work without Sanskrit words). | Constructed Hussain Sagar lake (built by Hussain Shah Wali) and Ibrahimpatnam tank; patronized Kuchipudi artists with royal land grants. |
| Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah (1580–1612 CE) | Founded the city of Hyderabad (Bhagyanagar) in 1591 CE; Urdu poet (Kulliyat-i-Quli Qutb Shah). | Maintained Telugu court poets; sponsored Telugu translations of medical texts. | Constructed the monumental Charminar (1591) and Mecca Masjid; granted trading privileges to Dutch and British at Machilipatnam. |
| Abul Hasan Tana Shah (1672–1687 CE) | Last Sultan; appointed Brahmin brothers Madanna as Prime Minister (Sur-i-Khel) and Akkanna as Commander-in-Chief. | Kancharla Gopanna (Bhakta Ramadasu), appointed Tahsildar of Bhadrachalam, constructed the Bhadrachalam Rama Temple using state revenues and composed soul-stirring 'Dasarathi Satakam'. | Heroically resisted 8-month Mughal siege by Aurangzeb until betrayed by Abdullah Khan Panni; Golconda annexed in 1687 CE. |



## 2. Salient Dimensions & Critical Analysis

* Accha Telugu Literature: Ponnikanti Telaganarya dedicated 'Yayati Charitra' to Amin Khan of Patancheru (Ibrahim Qutb Shah's minister), marking the first poetry written in pure indigenous Telugu (Accha Telugu) without borrowing a single Sanskrit loanword.

* Kuchipudi Dance Tradition: Siddhendra Yogi composed the dance-drama 'Bhamakalapam' (story of Satyabhama) at Kuchipudi village (Krishna district); Ibrahim Qutb Shah and later Abul Hasan Tana Shah granted Kuchipudi village as an Agraharam (1678) exclusively to Brahmin Bhagavatulu dance families.

* Bhakta Ramadasu (Kancharla Gopanna): Imprisoned for 12 years in Golconda Fort by Tana Shah for utilizing 6 lakh varahas of state tax to build the Bhadrachalam temple and jewel ornaments; legend relates Rama and Lakshmana appeared in disguise to pay his ransom; composed celebrated Kirtanas and 'Dasarathi Satakam'.

* Machilipatnam (Bandar): Primary maritime gateway of the Sultanate; exported kalamkari printed textiles, diamonds from Kollur mine (Hope Diamond, Koh-i-Noor), and steel for Damascus swords.

### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. Which Qutub Shahi Sultan of Golconda was affectionately venerated by Telugu poets as 'Malkibharama' for his enthusiastic patronage of classical Telugu literature?
(a) Sultan Quli Qutb Shah
(b) Ibrahim Qutb Shah
(c) Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah
(d) Abdullah Qutb Shah
Correct Answer: (b) Ibrahim Qutb Shah [Explanation: Ibrahim Qutb Shah, having spent seven years in exile at Vijayanagara, patronized Telugu poets who fondly hailed him as 'Malkibharama'.]

* Q2. The groundbreaking literary work 'Yayati Charitra', recognized as the very first poetic work composed entirely in pure indigenous Telugu (Accha Telugu), was authored by:
(a) Addanki Gangadhara Kavi
(b) Ponnikanti Telaganarya
(c) Sarangapani
(d) Kancharla Gopanna
Correct Answer: (b) Ponnikanti Telaganarya [Explanation: Ponnikanti Telaganarya composed 'Yayati Charitra' in pure Accha Telugu without Sanskrit loanwords, dedicating it to Amin Khan of Patancheru during Ibrahim Qutb Shah's reign.]

* Q3. The sacred dance-drama 'Bhamakalapam', which laid the foundational classical choreography for Kuchipudi dance in Andhra Pradesh, was composed by:
(a) Kshetrayya
(b) Siddhendra Yogi
(c) Tyagaraja
(d) Annamacharya
Correct Answer: (b) Siddhendra Yogi [Explanation: Siddhendra Yogi composed 'Bhamakalapam' (enacting the love and pride of Satyabhama) at Kuchipudi village, training young Brahmin boys in the classical dance-drama.]

* Q4. Which Qutub Shahi Sultan appointed the prominent Hindu Brahmin brothers Madanna as Prime Minister and Akkanna as Commander-in-Chief?
(a) Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah
(b) Abdullah Qutb Shah
(c) Abul Hasan Tana Shah
(d) Subhan Quli Qutb Shah
Correct Answer: (c) Abul Hasan Tana Shah [Explanation: Abul Hasan Tana Shah entrusted supreme civil and military governance to Madanna (Sur-i-Khel) and Akkanna, showcasing secular administration before the Mughal conquest in 1687.]

* Q5. Saint-composer Kancharla Gopanna (Bhakta Ramadasu), who composed the devotional 'Dasarathi Satakam' while imprisoned in Golconda Fort, served as the Tahsildar of:
(a) Kondapalli
(b) Bhadrachalam
(c) Khammam
(d) Warangal
Correct Answer: (b) Bhadrachalam [Explanation: Kancharla Gopanna served as the revenue collector (Tahsildar) of Bhadrachalam and constructed the famous Sri Sita Ramachandra Swamy Temple using state tax revenues.]
