# Musunuri Nayakas & Reddis of Kondavidu: Telugu Liberation & Golden Age of Poetry


Following the fall of Warangal to the Delhi Sultanate in 1323 CE, coastal Andhra and Telangana witnessed a heroic nationalist liberation movement led by the Musunuri Nayakas, who expelled the Tughlaq garrison. Concurrently, the Reddi kingdoms of Kondavidu and Rajahmundry established an independent renaissance of Telugu culture, celebrated for agricultural tank construction, Vasantotsava spring festivals, and the immortal poetry of Kavisarvabhauma Srinatha.

## 2. Salient Dimensions & Critical Analysis

* Vilasa Grant of Musunuri Prolaya Nayaka: Discovered in East Godavari; rare contemporary record lamenting the fall of Kakatiyas, the burning of villages and destruction of temples under Tughlaq armies, and celebrating Prolaya Nayaka as the savior who resurrected Dharma.

* Kavisarvabhauma Srinatha: Greatest Telugu poet of the medieval era; authored 'Haravilasa', 'Kasikhandam', 'Sringara Naishadham', 'Bhimakhandam', and the historical ballad 'Palnati Vira Charitra'; defeated Gauda Dindima Bhatta in the court of Devaraya II at Vijayanagara and was showered with molten gold (Kanakabhishekam).

* Yerrapragada (Errana): Court poet of Prolaya Vema Reddi; third of the Kavitrayam (Trinity of Telugu Poets); completed the unfinished Aranya Parva of Nannaya; authored 'Harivamsam' and 'Nrisimhapuranam'; honoured with titles 'Prabandha Parameshwara' and 'Sambhudasa'.

* Architectural Infrastructure: Built fortified hill strongholds at Kondavidu, Bellamkonda, Vinukonda, and Nagarjunakonda; constructed flight of stone steps (Sopanamarga) to the sacred hill shrines of Srisailam and Ahobilam.

### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. Which famous copper plate grant of Musunuri Prolaya Nayaka provides an authentic historical narrative of the liberation of Andhra from Delhi Sultanate rule?
(a) Motupalli Grant
(b) Vilasa Grant
(c) Kondavidu Grant
(d) Draksharama Grant
Correct Answer: (b) Vilasa Grant [Explanation: The Vilasa copper plate grant of Prolaya Nayaka describes the devastation caused by the Tughlaqs and details the confederacy of Nayakas that liberated the Telugu country.]

* Q2. Which Reddi monarch of Kondavidu was celebrated with the title 'Karpura Vasantaraya' for his patronage of music, dance, and annual spring festivals?
(a) Prolaya Vema Reddi
(b) Anavota Reddi
(c) Kumaragiri Reddi
(d) Racha Vema Reddi
Correct Answer: (c) Kumaragiri Reddi [Explanation: Kumaragiri Reddi authored the Sanskrit drama treatise 'Vasantarajiya' and celebrated lavish Vasantotsavas, earning the title 'Karpura Vasantaraya'.]

* Q3. The great Telugu poet laureate Kavisarvabhauma Srinatha was bathed in gold (Kanakabhishekam) in the Pearl Hall (Muthyala Shala) of which emperor's court?
(a) Krishnadevaraya
(b) Devaraya II
(c) Harihara II
(d) Kumaragiri Reddi
Correct Answer: (b) Devaraya II [Explanation: Srinatha defeated royal court scholar Dindima Bhatta in debate and was honoured with Kanakabhishekam by Vijayanagar Emperor Devaraya II in Hampi.]

* Q4. Which poet among the 'Kavitrayam' (Trinity of Telugu Poets) was patronized by Prolaya Vema Reddi and completed the unfinished Aranya Parva of the Mahabharata?
(a) Nannaya
(b) Tikkana
(c) Yerrapragada (Errana)
(d) Srinatha
Correct Answer: (c) Yerrapragada (Errana) [Explanation: Errana (Yerrapragada), court poet of Prolaya Vema Reddi, completed the half-finished Aranya Parva left by Nannaya, earning the title 'Prabandha Parameshwara'.]

* Q5. Which historic hill fortress served as the impregnable capital of the Reddi kingdom in Guntur district before its capture by the Vijayanagar Empire?
(a) Kondapalli Fort
(b) Kondavidu Fort
(c) Chandragiri Fort
(d) Gandikota Fort
Correct Answer: (b) Kondavidu Fort [Explanation: Kondavidu Fort in Guntur was the formidable capital of the Reddi rulers, renowned for its triple ramparts, water reservoirs, and granaries.]
