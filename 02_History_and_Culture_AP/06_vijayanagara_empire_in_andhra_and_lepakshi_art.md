# Vijayanagara in Andhra: Rayalaseema Zenith, Lepakshi Art & Telugu Literature


The Vijayanagar Empire held a deeply intimate connection with the Andhra region, particularly the Rayalaseema hinterland. Under Emperor Sri Krishnadevaraya (1509–1529 CE), Telugu literature attained its classical 'Prabandha' zenith with the court circle of 'Ashtadiggajas'. Concurrently, Andhra architecture reached supreme expressive heights in the hanging pillars and vibrant fresco murals of the Lepakshi Veerabhadra Temple, while Penukonda and Chandragiri served as imperial redoubts following the Battle of Talikota.

## 2. Salient Dimensions & Critical Analysis

* Amuktamalyada: Masterpiece composed by Krishnadevaraya detailing the divine marriage of Lord Ranganatha with Tamil Alvar poetess Goda Devi (Andal); contains the famous fourth canto on political statecraft, diplomacy, and righteous governance (Rajaniti).

* Ashtadiggajas Masterpieces: Allasani Peddana ('Manucharitra'), Nandi Timmana ('Parijatapaharanam'), Tenali Ramakrishna ('Panduranga Mahatmyam'), Dhurjati ('Kalahastiswara Mahatmyam'), Pingali Surana ('Kalapurnodayam' - world's first novelistic prabandha).

* Lepakshi Hanging Pillar: Architectural marvel among 70 pillars in the Natya Mandapa that does not touch the stone floor, allowing thin cloth or paper to pass underneath.

* Tallapaka Annamacharya (1408–1503 CE): The 'Pada Kavita Pitamaha'; contemporary saint-composer who composed 32,000 sringara and sankirtana padas in Telugu dedicated to Lord Venkateswara of Tirupati, engraved on copper plates stored in the Sankeertana Bhandagaram.

### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. Which architectural marvel inside the Natya Mandapa of the Lepakshi Veerabhadra Temple is renowned for not resting entirely on the ground floor?
(a) Monolithic Nandi
(b) The Hanging Pillar
(c) Nagalinga
(d) Kalyana Mandapa
Correct Answer: (b) The Hanging Pillar [Explanation: The Hanging Pillar of Lepakshi is a famed stone column suspended slightly above the temple floor, demonstrating Vijayanagar engineering precision.]

* Q2. In his Telugu epic 'Amuktamalyada', Sri Krishnadevaraya famously declared which linguistic tribute to the Telugu language?
(a) 'Telugu Talli Chirunavvu'
(b) 'Desa Bhashalandu Telugu Lessa'
(c) 'Andhra Bhasha Amrutham'
(d) 'Vaganusasana Telugu'
Correct Answer: (b) 'Desa Bhashalandu Telugu Lessa' [Explanation: In the introduction to 'Amuktamalyada', Krishnadevaraya proclaimed 'Desa Bhashalandu Telugu Lessa' (Among the languages of the nation, Telugu is the best).]

* Q3. Who among the 'Ashtadiggajas' authored the celebrated poetic work 'Parijatapaharanam' to reconcile a domestic lovers' tiff between Krishnadevaraya and Queen Tirumala Devi?
(a) Allasani Peddana
(b) Nandi Timmana
(c) Tenali Ramakrishna
(d) Madayyagari Mallana
Correct Answer: (b) Nandi Timmana [Explanation: Mukku Timmana (Nandi Timmana) composed 'Parijatapaharanam' (The Theft of the Parijata Flower), presenting the charming mythological tale to pacify royal marital discord.]

* Q4. Which saint-composer, revered as 'Pada Kavita Pitamaha', composed 32,000 devotional sankirtanas in Telugu dedicated to Lord Venkateswara of Tirumala?
(a) Tyagaraja
(b) Tallapaka Annamacharya
(c) Kancharla Gopanna (Ramadasu)
(d) Kshetriyya
Correct Answer: (b) Tallapaka Annamacharya [Explanation: Tallapaka Annamacharya (1408–1503 CE) composed 32,000 padas inscribed on copper plates preserved in the Tirumala temple, pioneering the Telugu Sankirtana tradition.]

* Q5. Which historic fort in Andhra Pradesh served as the final imperial capital of the Vijayanagar Aravidu dynasty, where British envoy Francis Day secured the charter for Madras in 1639?
(a) Gooty Fort
(b) Chandragiri Fort
(c) Penukonda Fort
(d) Udayagiri Fort
Correct Answer: (b) Chandragiri Fort [Explanation: Chandragiri Fort near Tirupati served as the seat of Emperor Venkata II and Damarla Chennappa Nayaka, who leased Madraspatnam to the English EIC in 1639.]
