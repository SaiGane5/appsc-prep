# Quit India Movement, Subhas Chandra Bose & Transfer of Power


The 1940s brought the climactic denouement of the Indian freedom struggle. Triggered by the failure of the Cripps Mission (1942), the Quit India Movement unleashed an unprecedented spontaneous mass rebellion under the clarion call 'Do or Die'. Simultaneously, Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose's Indian National Army (INA) challenged British arms across Southeast Asia and the Manipur frontier, precipitating post-war naval mutinies that convinced the British that transfer of power was unavoidable.

## 1. Institutional & Structural Framework


### Table: Milestones in the Final Transition to Indian Independence

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 2. Salient Dimensions & Critical Analysis

* August Offer (8 August 1940, Viceroy Linlithgow): Promised Dominion status in unspecified future and expansion of Viceroy's Executive Council; rejected by Congress, which launched Individual Satyagraha (first satyagrahi: Vinoba Bhave, second: Jawaharlal Nehru).

* Underground Movement in 1942: Aruna Asaf Ali hoisted the national flag at Gowalia Tank; Usha Mehta operated the clandestine 'Congress Radio' broadcasting from Bombay; Jayaprakash Narayan escaped Hazaribagh jail to organize Azad Dasta guerrilla squads.

* Direct Action Day (16 August 1946): Muslim League under Jinnah launched Direct Action Day to demand Pakistan, unleashing catastrophic communal pogroms ('Great Calcutta Killings') spreading to Noakhali and Bihar, where Gandhi walked barefoot on peace missions.

* Radcliffe Line: Sir Cyril Radcliffe chaired the Boundary Commissions that partitioned Punjab and Bengal into India and Pakistan, dividing communities with hurried cartographic borders.

### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. At the Gowalia Tank Maidan in Bombay on 8 August 1942, Mahatma Gandhi delivered which historic clarion call to the nation while launching the Quit India Movement?
(a) 'Swaraj is my birthright'
(b) 'Do or Die' (Karo ya Maro)
(c) 'Inquilab Zindabad'
(d) 'Give me blood, and I shall give you freedom'
Correct Answer: (b) 'Do or Die' (Karo ya Maro) [Explanation: Gandhi gave the memorable mantra 'Do or Die' at Gowalia Tank on 8 August 1942, declaring: 'We shall either free India or die in the attempt.']

* Q2. Who operated an underground clandestine radio transmission network ('Secret Congress Radio') during the Quit India Movement of 1942 to broadcast uncensored news?
(a) Aruna Asaf Ali
(b) Usha Mehta
(c) Sucheta Kripalani
(d) Sarojini Naidu
Correct Answer: (b) Usha Mehta [Explanation: Dr. Usha Mehta and her associates set up the clandestine 'Congress Radio' broadcasting patriotic bulletins from shifting locations across Bombay during 1942.]

* Q3. Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose established the Provisional Government of Free India ('Arzi Hukumat-e-Azad Hind') in exile on 21 October 1943 in:
(a) Tokyo
(b) Berlin
(c) Singapore
(d) Rangoon
Correct Answer: (c) Singapore [Explanation: Netaji proclaimed the Provisional Government of Free India (Arzi Hukumat-e-Azad Hind) at Cathay Cinema hall in Singapore on 21 October 1943.]

* Q4. Which all-women combat regiment was created within the Indian National Army (INA) under the commanding leadership of Captain Lakshmi Sahgal?
(a) Jhansi Ki Rani Regiment
(b) Kasturba Regiment
(c) Sarojini Naidu Brigade
(d) Durga Bhabhi Battalion
Correct Answer: (a) Jhansi Ki Rani Regiment [Explanation: Netaji raised the pioneering all-women combat unit 'Rani of Jhansi Regiment' in 1943, commanded by Captain Lakshmi Swaminathan (Sahgal).]

* Q5. The Royal Indian Navy (RIN) Mutiny of February 1946, which signaled the final collapse of military loyalty to the British Raj, commenced on which naval ratings ship in Bombay?
(a) HMIS Vikrant
(b) HMIS Talwar
(c) HMIS Shivaji
(d) HMIS Ganga
Correct Answer: (b) HMIS Talwar [Explanation: On 18 February 1946, 1,100 naval ratings aboard the signal training ship HMIS Talwar in Bombay went on strike against racial discrimination, unpalatable food, and the abuse of Indian sailors.]
