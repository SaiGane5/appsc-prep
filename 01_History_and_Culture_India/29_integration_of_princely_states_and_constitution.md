# Integration of Princely States & The Making of the Indian Constitution


On the eve of independence in August 1947, India faced the existential threat of Balkanization into over 565 autonomous princely fiefdoms following the lapse of British Paramountcy. Through the consummate diplomatic finesse, statesmanship, and firm resolve of Deputy Prime Minister Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel and States Secretary V.P. Menon, the princely states were successfully integrated into the Indian Union, while the Constituent Assembly under Dr. B.R. Ambedkar framed the world's most comprehensive written democratic constitution.

## 2. Salient Dimensions & Critical Analysis

* Instruments of Accession & Privy Purses: Rulers surrendered Defence, Foreign Affairs, and Communications to the Indian Union; granted transitional tax-free Privy Purses and royal titles (abolished in 1971 by the 26th Constitutional Amendment under Indira Gandhi).

* Constituent Assembly Composition: 389 members initially (299 post-partition) elected indirectly by Provincial Assemblies; First Meeting on 9 December 1946 (Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha interim President; Dr. Rajendra Prasad elected permanent President on 11 Dec); B.N. Rau appointed Constitutional Advisor.

* Objectives Resolution (13 December 1946): Moved by Jawaharlal Nehru; laid down ideological foundation of an Independent Sovereign Republic committed to justice, liberty, and equality; adopted on 22 January 1947; formed the basis of the Preamble.

* Drafting Committee: Appointed on 29 August 1947 under the chairmanship of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar ('Chief Architect of the Constitution'); 7 members (Ambedkar, N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar, Alladi Krishnaswamy Iyer, K.M. Munshi, Mohammad Saadulla, B.L. Mitter / N. Madhava Rau, D.P. Khaitan / T.T. Krishnamachari).

* Adoption & Commencement: Took 2 years, 11 months, and 18 days across 11 sessions; adopted on 26 November 1949 (celebrated as National Constitution Day); came into full legal effect on 26 January 1950 (Republic Day).

### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. Which civil servant served as the Secretary of the Ministry of States and worked in close partnership with Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel in negotiating the integration of 565 princely states?
(a) V.P. Menon
(b) B.N. Rau
(c) H.M. Patel
(d) G.S. Bajpai
Correct Answer: (a) V.P. Menon [Explanation: V.P. Menon, Secretary of the Ministry of States under Sardar Patel, drafted the Instrument of Accession and handled delicate diplomatic negotiations with princely rulers.]

* Q2. The military police action launched by the Indian Army in September 1948 to liberate and integrate the princely state of Hyderabad into the Indian Union was codenamed:
(a) Operation Vijay
(b) Operation Polo
(c) Operation Cactus
(d) Operation Blue Star
Correct Answer: (b) Operation Polo [Explanation: Operation Polo (13–18 September 1948) was the military action commanded by Maj. Gen. J.N. Chaudhuri that forced the surrender of Nizam Mir Osman Ali Khan and dismantled the Razakar militia.]

* Q3. Who served as the Constitutional Advisor to the Constituent Assembly, preparing the initial foundational draft of the Indian Constitution?
(a) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
(b) Sir B.N. Rau
(c) Alladi Krishnaswamy Iyer
(d) K.M. Munshi
Correct Answer: (b) Sir B.N. Rau [Explanation: Sir Benegal Narsing Rau (B.N. Rau), eminent jurist and judge of the International Court of Justice, served as the Constitutional Advisor to the Constituent Assembly.]

* Q4. On which historic date was the Constitution of India formally adopted by the Constituent Assembly, celebrated annually across India as 'Samvidhan Diwas' (Constitution Day)?
(a) 15 August 1947
(b) 26 November 1949
(c) 26 January 1950
(d) 2 October 1950
Correct Answer: (b) 26 November 1949 [Explanation: The Constituent Assembly adopted the Constitution on 26 November 1949 (commemorated as National Constitution Day), while it came into full force on 26 January 1950.]

* Q5. The famous 'Objectives Resolution', which articulated the philosophy and guiding ideals of the Indian Republic and formed the Preamble, was moved in the Constituent Assembly by:
(a) Dr. Rajendra Prasad
(b) Jawaharlal Nehru
(c) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
(d) Maulana Abul Kalam Azad
Correct Answer: (b) Jawaharlal Nehru [Explanation: Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru moved the historic Objectives Resolution on 13 December 1946, which was unanimously adopted on 22 January 1947 and shaped the Preamble.]
