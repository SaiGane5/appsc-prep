# E-Governance Initiatives & Civil Service Reforms: 2nd ARC & Digital India


Administrative modernization in India is driven by the transformative convergence of Digital Governance and structural civil service reforms. Moving from traditional paper-driven bureaucracy to algorithm-enabled digital delivery platforms (Digital India, JAM Trinity, e-Office), the state enhances speed, eliminates leakage, and improves citizen access, guided by the foundational recommendations of the Second Administrative Reforms Commission (2nd ARC).

## 1. Institutional & Structural Framework

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Digital India Mission (2015) | Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY). | Nine pillars: broadband highways, universal mobile access, public internet, e-governance, e-kranti, information for all, electronics manufacturing, IT for jobs, early harvest. | Created digital public infrastructure (DPI / India Stack: Aadhaar, UPI, DigiLocker, CoWIN). |

| Grama / Ward Sachivalayam (AP Model) | Government of Andhra Pradesh. | Over 15,000 decentralized village/ward secretariats staffed by 134,000 functional employees supported by 260,000 ward volunteers. | Faceless, door-step delivery of 540+ citizen welfare services within fixed Citizen Charter timelines. |



## 2. Salient Dimensions & Critical Analysis

* Second Administrative Reforms Commission (2nd ARC, 2005–2009): Chaired by Veerappa Moily; submitted 15 comprehensive reports covering 'Ethics in Governance', 'Unlocking Human Capital', 'Citizen Centric Administration', 'Local Governance', and 'Promoting e-Governance'.

* Key 2nd ARC Civil Service Recommendations: Introduce performance-related incentives, periodic mid-career reviews at 15 and 20 years with premature retirement for deadwood, lateral entry of domain specialists at Joint Secretary level, statutory Civil Services Code with code of ethics, and independent Civil Services Board.

* Mission Karmayogi (National Programme for Civil Services Capacity Building - NPCSCB): Digital competency-based training platform (iGOT Karmayogi) shifting civil servants from 'Rule-based' to 'Role-based' performance.

* National Data Sharing and Accessibility Policy (NDSAP) & DigiLocker: Promotes open government data (data.gov.in) and cloud-based verification of official documents without physical notarization.

### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. Who served as the Chairman of the Second Administrative Reforms Commission (2nd ARC) constituted by the Government of India in 2005?
(a) K. Kasturirangan
(b) M. Veerappa Moily
(c) Bimal Jalan
(d) Montek Singh Ahluwalia
Correct Answer: (b) M. Veerappa Moily [Explanation: The Second ARC was appointed in August 2005 under the chairmanship of M. Veerappa Moily (later succeeded by V. Ramachandran) and produced 15 landmark reports.]

* Q2. 'Mission Karmayogi', launched by the Union Cabinet in September 2020, is a comprehensive institutional mission designed for:
(a) Rural electrification infrastructure
(b) Civil Services Capacity Building and competency training
(c) Tribal education scholarship disbursement
(d) Micro-irrigation management in drylands
Correct Answer: (b) Civil Services Capacity Building and competency training [Explanation: Mission Karmayogi (NPCSCB) focuses on modernizing civil service competencies through the digital iGOT-Karmayogi platform, fostering role-based governance.]

* Q3. The centralized online grievance redressal platform operated by the Government of India to register citizen complaints across central ministries is known as:
(a) PRAGATI
(b) CPGRAMS
(c) e-Taal
(d) UMANG
Correct Answer: (b) CPGRAMS [Explanation: CPGRAMS (Centralised Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System) is the 24x7 online single-window grievance portal managed by DARPG.]

* Q4. Which acronym is universally used to describe the digital financial inclusion architecture combining basic bank accounts, biometric identification, and mobile connectivity in India?
(a) UPI Trinity
(b) JAM Trinity (Jan Dhan-Aadhaar-Mobile)
(c) DBT Matrix
(d) NeGP Grid
Correct Answer: (b) JAM Trinity (Jan Dhan-Aadhaar-Mobile) [Explanation: The JAM Trinity (Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana, Aadhaar, and Mobile connectivity) provides the technological bedrock for Direct Benefit Transfers.]

* Q5. Which innovative decentralized governance model was introduced in Andhra Pradesh to provide doorstep delivery of over 500 citizen services via 15,000 local secretariats?
(a) MeeSeva Portal
(b) Grama / Ward Sachivalayam System
(c) e-Seva Kendra
(d) Praja Vedika
Correct Answer: (b) Grama / Ward Sachivalayam System [Explanation: The Grama/Ward Sachivalayam system in Andhra Pradesh deployed over 15,000 secretariats staffed by local functionaries and volunteers to provide localized public service delivery.]
