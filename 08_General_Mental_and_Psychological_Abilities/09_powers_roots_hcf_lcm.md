# Exponents, Radicals, Surds, Square/Cube Roots, and HCF-LCM Mathematics


Exponential arithmetic and fundamental prime factor extraction form the mechanical toolkit for rapid mathematical calculations. Understanding index laws and the structural product relationship between HCF and LCM solves complex cyclical synchronization and grouping problems.


## 1. Laws of Indices & Surd Operations


### Table: Fundamental Laws of Exponents and Radicals

| Index Law | Mathematical Formulation | Example & Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product of Powers | $a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$ | $2^3 \times 2^4 = 2^7 = 128$ |
| Quotient of Powers | $\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}$ | $\frac{5^6}{5^2} = 5^4 = 625$ |
| Power of a Power | $(a^m)^n = a^{mn}$ | $(3^2)^3 = 3^6 = 729$ |
| Negative & Zero Exponents | $a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}$, $a^0 = 1$ ($a \ne 0$) | $4^{-2} = \frac{1}{16}$, $7^0 = 1$ |
| Fractional Exponents | $a^{1/n} = \sqrt[n]{a}$, $a^{m/n} = (\sqrt[n]{a})^m$ | $27^{2/3} = (\sqrt[3]{27})^2 = 3^2 = 9$ |



## 2. HCF and LCM Formulations & Golden Product Rule

* Highest Common Factor (HCF / GCD): The greatest divisor common to two or more numbers. Calculated by prime factorization taking the lowest power of all common prime factors.

* Least Common Multiple (LCM): The smallest non-zero positive integer divisible by all given numbers. Calculated taking the highest power of every prime factor involved.

* Golden Product Rule: For any two positive integers A and B: $\text{HCF}(A, B) \times \text{LCM}(A, B) = A \times B$.

* HCF & LCM of Fractions: $\text{HCF of Fractions} = \frac{\text{HCF of Numerators}}{\text{LCM of Denominators}}$, and $\text{LCM of Fractions} = \frac{\text{LCM of Numerators}}{\text{HCF of Denominators}}$.

### Part A: Analytical Synchronization Problem

1. Four electronic traffic signals at four distinct intersections in Vijayawada change their lights every 30 seconds, 45 seconds, 60 seconds, and 90 seconds respectively. If all four signals change simultaneously at 8:00 AM, at what time will they next change simultaneously? Formulate the LCM prime factorization model. (APPSC Group-1 Mains Analytical, 10 Marks)
-> Model Approach: Simultaneous synchronization occurs at the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of the individual cycle intervals. Given intervals: 30s, 45s, 60s, 90s.
Prime factorization:
30 = 2^1 * 3^1 * 5^1
45 = 3^2 * 5^1
60 = 2^2 * 3^1 * 5^1
90 = 2^1 * 3^2 * 5^1
LCM = 2^max(1,0,2,1) * 3^max(1,2,1,2) * 5^max(1,1,1,1) = 2^2 * 3^2 * 5^1 = 4 * 9 * 5 = 180 seconds.
Convert seconds to minutes: 180 seconds / 60 = 3 minutes.
Therefore, the signals will next change simultaneously at 8:00 AM + 3 minutes = 8:03 AM.




### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. The HCF of two numbers is 11 and their LCM is 7700. If one of the numbers is 275, what is the other number?
(a) 289
(b) 308
(c) 318
(d) 328
Correct Answer: (b) 308 [Explanation: Product of two numbers = HCF * LCM. 275 * Other = 11 * 7700 => Other = (11 * 7700) / 275 = 7700 / 25 = 308.]

* Q2. What is the HCF of fractions 2/3, 8/9, 16/81, and 10/27?
(a) 2/81
(b) 80/3
(c) 2/3
(d) 16/81
Correct Answer: (a) 2/81 [Explanation: HCF of fractions = HCF(Numerators) / LCM(Denominators) = HCF(2, 8, 16, 10) / LCM(3, 9, 81, 27) = 2 / 81.]

* Q3. What is the square root of 0.00059049?
(a) 0.00243
(b) 0.0243
(c) 0.243
(d) 0.000243
Correct Answer: (b) 0.0243 [Explanation: 59049 = 243^2. The number has 8 decimal places, so its square root will have 8 / 2 = 4 decimal places, which gives 0.0243.]

* Q4. If 2^(x - 1) + 2^(x + 1) = 320, find the value of x.
(a) 5
(b) 6
(c) 7
(d) 8
Correct Answer: (c) 7 [Explanation: 2^(x-1) + 2^(x+1) = 2^x * (1/2 + 2) = 2^x * (5/2) = 320 => 2^x = (320 * 2) / 5 = 64 * 2 = 128 = 2^7 => x = 7.]

* Q5. What is the largest 4-digit number that is completely divisible by 12, 15, 18, and 27?
(a) 9690
(b) 9720
(c) 9810
(d) 9920
Correct Answer: (b) 9720 [Explanation: LCM of 12, 15, 18, 27 = 540. Largest 4-digit number is 9999. Dividing 9999 by 540 gives quotient 18 with remainder 279. Largest required number = 9999 - 279 = 9720.]
