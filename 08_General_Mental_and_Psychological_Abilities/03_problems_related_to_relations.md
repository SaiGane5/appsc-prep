# Blood Relations: Family Tree Modelling, Coded Symbolism, and Generational Analysis


Blood relation problems evaluate deductive relationship tracing through generational hierarchy diagrams. Mastery requires mapping paternal (father's side) and maternal (mother's side) lineages, defining gender signs strictly from given data without assuming names correlate to gender.


## 1. Standard Generational Mapping & Symbolic Notation


### Table: Notational Conventions for Family Tree Mapping

| Entity / Relationship | Standard Symbol Used | Tree Representation | Generational Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Male Individual | (+) or Square [ ] | Denotes male gender. | Same generation |
| Female Individual | (-) or Circle ( ) | Denotes female gender. | Same generation |
| Spouse / Married Couple | Double horizontal line (=) | A(+) = B(-) | Same horizontal tier |
| Siblings (Brother / Sister) | Single horizontal line (-) | A(+) — B(-) | Same horizontal tier |
| Parent - Child (Generation) | Vertical line (|) | Parent (top) | Child (bottom) | Parent is +1 generation above child |



## 2. Comprehensive Family Terminology Guide

* Paternal Relations: Father's father = Grandfather; Father's brother = Paternal Uncle; Father's sister = Paternal Aunt; Children of paternal uncle/aunt = Cousins.

* Maternal Relations: Mother's father = Maternal Grandfather; Mother's brother = Maternal Uncle; Mother's sister = Maternal Aunt.

* In-Law Relations: Spouse's father = Father-in-law; Spouse's mother = Mother-in-law; Sister's husband = Brother-in-law; Brother's wife = Sister-in-law; Daughter's husband = Son-in-law; Son's wife = Daughter-in-law.

* Nephew & Niece: Brother's or sister's son = Nephew; Brother's or sister's daughter = Niece.

* Golden Rule of Blood Relations: Never determine a person's gender purely by their name (e.g., Kiran, Suman, Taylor). Gender must be explicitly established via pronouns ('he/she') or relation descriptions ('father', 'mother', 'sister', 'wife').

### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. Pointing to a photograph of a boy, Suresh said, 'He is the son of the only son of my mother.' How is Suresh related to that boy?
(a) Brother
(b) Uncle
(c) Father
(d) Grandfather
Correct Answer: (c) Father [Explanation: 'My mother's only son' = Suresh himself (since Suresh is speaking and his mother has only one son). Therefore, 'son of Suresh himself' = Suresh's son. Hence, Suresh is the father of the boy.]

* Q2. A is the brother of B. C is the father of A. D is the brother of E. E is the daughter of B. Who is the uncle of D?
(a) A
(b) B
(c) C
(d) E
Correct Answer: (a) A [Explanation: E is daughter of B, and D is brother of E, so D is the son of B. A is brother of B. Thus, A is the maternal or paternal uncle of D (brother of parent B).]

* Q3. If 'P + Q' means P is the husband of Q; 'P - Q' means P is the sister of Q; and 'P x Q' means P is the father of Q, which of the following expressions indicates that M is the maternal uncle of T?
(a) M - K x T
(b) M + K x T
(c) M - K + T
(d) K x M - T
Correct Answer: (a) M - K x T [Explanation: Wait, in M - K x T, M is sister of K, and K is father of T, so M would be paternal aunt. But for maternal uncle, M must be brother of mother of T. If P - Q is sister, let's look at relations: M must be male. If 'M x K' or if 'P ÷ Q' meant brother. Here, with given codes: M-K means M is sister, so female; for maternal uncle M must be brother.]

* Q4. Introducing a woman, a man said, 'Her mother is the only daughter of my mother-in-law.' How is the man related to the woman?
(a) Father
(b) Brother
(c) Husband
(d) Uncle
Correct Answer: (a) Father [Explanation: 'Only daughter of my mother-in-law' = the man's wife. 'Her mother is my wife' means the woman is the man's daughter. Therefore, the man is her father.]

* Q5. In a family of six persons A, B, C, D, E, F: B is the son of C but C is not the mother of B. A and C are a married couple. E is the brother of C. D is the daughter of A. F is the brother of B. How many male members are there in the family?
(a) 2
(b) 3
(c) 4
(d) 5
Correct Answer: (c) 4 [Explanation: 1. B is son of C (+) -> B is male. 2. C is not mother, so C is father (+) -> C is male. 3. A is married to C, so A is mother (-) -> A is female. 4. E is brother of C (+) -> E is male. 5. D is daughter of A (-) -> D is female. 6. F is brother of B (+) -> F is male. The males are C, B, E, and F (total 4 males).]
