## 1. Angle Relationships & Parallel Transversal Properties


### Table: Angle Theorems Formed by Transversals Intersecting Parallel Lines

| Angle Category | Geometric Position | Theoretic Property |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Complementary Angles | Two angles whose sum is exactly $90^\circ$. | $\angle A + \angle B = 90^\circ$ |
| Supplementary Angles | Two angles whose sum is exactly $180^\circ$ (Linear pair). | $\angle A + \angle B = 180^\circ$ |
| Corresponding Angles | Same relative position at each intersection. | Equal in measure (e.g., $\angle 1 = \angle 5$) |
| Alternate Interior Angles | Opposite sides of transversal between parallel lines. | Equal in measure (form a 'Z' shape, $\angle 3 = \angle 5$) |
| Consecutive Interior Angles | Same side of transversal between parallel lines. | Supplementary: sum is $180^\circ$ ($\angle 3 + \angle 6 = 180^\circ$) |



## 2. Quadrilateral Taxonomy & Polygon Angle Formulas

* Sum of Interior Angles of an $n$-sided Polygon: $S_i = (n - 2) \times 180^\circ$. Each interior angle in a regular polygon is $\frac{(n-2) \times 180^\circ}{n}$.

* Sum of Exterior Angles of ANY Convex Polygon: Always strictly equals $360^\circ$. Each exterior angle in a regular polygon is $\frac{360^\circ}{n}$.

* Parallelogram: Opposite sides are equal and parallel; opposite angles are equal; consecutive angles are supplementary ($180^\circ$); diagonals bisect each other (but are NOT necessarily equal or perpendicular).

* Rhombus: All 4 sides equal; opposite sides parallel; diagonals bisect each other perpendicularly at $90^\circ$ (act as angle bisectors).

* Rectangle: A parallelogram with all four angles equal to $90^\circ$; diagonals are equal in length and bisect each other.




## Previous Years Questions (PYQs) - APPSC Group-1


### Part A: Analytical Geometric Deductions

1. Prove that the quadrilateral formed by joining the mid-points of the sides of any arbitrary quadrilateral is always a parallelogram (Varignon's Theorem). Formulate the proof using triangle mid-point theorems. (APPSC Group-1 Mains Analytical, 10 Marks)
-> Model Approach: Let ABCD be an arbitrary quadrilateral and P, Q, R, S be the mid-points of sides AB, BC, CD, and DA respectively. Join diagonal AC.
1. In triangle ABC: P is mid-point of AB, Q is mid-point of BC. By Mid-point Theorem, PQ is parallel to AC and PQ = (1/2) AC.
2. In triangle ADC: S is mid-point of AD, R is mid-point of CD. By Mid-point Theorem, SR is parallel to AC and SR = (1/2) AC.
3. From (1) and (2): PQ is parallel to SR, and PQ = SR.
4. A quadrilateral with one pair of opposite sides both equal and parallel is by definition a parallelogram. Therefore, PQRS is a parallelogram.




### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. Each interior angle of a regular polygon is 144°. How many sides does the polygon possess?
(a) 8
(b) 9
(c) 10
(d) 12
Correct Answer: (c) 10 [Explanation: Each exterior angle = 180° - 144° = 36°. Since the sum of exterior angles of any regular polygon is 360°, number of sides n = 360° / 36° = 10 sides (a decagon).]

* Q2. In a triangle ABC, the bisectors of angle B and angle C intersect at an internal point O. If angle A = 70°, what is the measure of angle BOC?
(a) 110°
(b) 125°
(c) 135°
(d) 140°
Correct Answer: (b) 125° [Explanation: Angle at incenter O is given by: ∠BOC = 90° + (∠A / 2) = 90° + (70° / 2) = 90° + 35° = 125°.]

* Q3. The diagonals of a rhombus are 16 cm and 12 cm. What is the perimeter of the rhombus?
(a) 28 cm
(b) 40 cm
(c) 48 cm
(d) 56 cm
Correct Answer: (b) 40 cm [Explanation: Diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other perpendicularly at 90°. Half lengths = 8 cm and 6 cm. By Pythagorean theorem: side a = √(8^2 + 6^2) = √(64 + 36) = √100 = 10 cm. Perimeter = 4a = 4 * 10 = 40 cm.]

* Q4. If two parallel lines are intersected by a transversal, and one of the interior angles on the same side is (2x + 15)° while the other is (3x + 10)°, find the value of x.
(a) 25°
(b) 31°
(c) 35°
(d) 40°
Correct Answer: (b) 31° [Explanation: Consecutive interior angles on the same side of a transversal are supplementary (sum = 180°). (2x + 15) + (3x + 10) = 180 => 5x + 25 = 180 => 5x = 155 => x = 31°.]

* Q5. An angle is 24° more than its complement. What is the measure of the angle?
(a) 48°
(b) 52°
(c) 57°
(d) 66°
Correct Answer: (c) 57° [Explanation: Let the angle be x. Its complement is (90 - x). x = (90 - x) + 24 => 2x = 114 => x = 57°.]
