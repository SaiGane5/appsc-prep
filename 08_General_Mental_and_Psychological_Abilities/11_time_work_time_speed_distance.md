# Time & Work Dynamics, Efficiency Modelling, and Kinematics of Speed, Distance & Relative Motion


Time-Work and Time-Distance problems evaluate understanding of inverse rates of variation. The LCM efficiency approach simplifies complex multi-agent productivity schedules, while relative velocity resolves linear vehicular crossings and aquatic currents.


## 1. Time & Work: The LCM Total Work & Efficiency Method


### Table: Time and Work Rate Relationships

| Entity / Principle | Mathematical Rule | Operational Shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total Work Formulation | $\text{Total Work} = \text{LCM}(\text{Individual Days})$ | Eliminates fractional calculations by expressing work in integer 'units'. |
| Individual Daily Efficiency | $\text{Efficiency} = \frac{\text{Total Work}}{\text{Days Taken}}$ | Units of work executed per day. |
| Two-Person Combined Work | If A takes $x$ days and B takes $y$ days. | $\text{Time Together} = \frac{xy}{x + y}$ days. |
| Pipes & Cisterns (Negative Work) | Inlet pipe adds positive efficiency; leak/outlet pipe subtracts negative efficiency. | $\text{Net Rate} = \text{Inlet Rate} - \text{Outlet Rate}$ |



## 2. Time, Speed & Distance: Relative Velocities

* Unit Conversion: To convert from km/h to m/s, multiply by $5/18$. To convert from m/s to km/h, multiply by $18/5$.

* Average Speed for Equal Distances: Harmonic mean formula: $\text{Average Speed} = \frac{2xy}{x+y}$ (where $x$ and $y$ are outbound and inbound speeds).

* Relative Speed: If two bodies move in the SAME direction, $\text{Relative Speed} = |v_1 - v_2|$. If they move in OPPOSITE directions, $\text{Relative Speed} = v_1 + v_2$.

* Train Crossing Formulas: Crossing a point/pole: $\text{Time} = \frac{L_{\text{train}}}{v}$. Crossing a platform of length $P$: $\text{Time} = \frac{L_{\text{train}} + P}{v}$.

* Boats and Streams: Downstream speed $D = u + v$ (boat speed + stream speed). Upstream speed $U = u - v$. Boat speed in still water $u = \frac{D + U}{2}$; Stream speed $v = \frac{D - U}{2}$.




## Previous Years Questions (PYQs) - APPSC Group-1


### Part A: Analytical Kinematics & Work Optimization

1. A and B can complete a project in 12 days, B and C in 15 days, and C and A in 20 days. How long will they take to complete the project working all together? If A leaves after 4 days of joint work, in how many more days will B and C finish the remainder? Formulate the unitary efficiency breakdown. (APPSC Group-1 Mains Analytical, 10 Marks)
-> Model Approach: 1. Total Work = LCM(12, 15, 20) = 60 units. 2. Efficiency equations: (A + B) = 60/12 = 5 units/day; (B + C) = 60/15 = 4 units/day; (C + A) = 60/20 = 3 units/day. Sum of equations: 2(A + B + C) = 5 + 4 + 3 = 12 => Combined (A + B + C) = 6 units/day. Days together = 60 / 6 = 10 days. 3. Remaining work: In first 4 days together, work done = 4 * 6 = 24 units. Remaining work = 60 - 24 = 36 units. Since A leaves, B and C continue together with combined rate of 4 units/day. Additional time required = 36 / 4 = 9 days.




### Part B: Screening / Prelims Objective Questions

* Q1. A train 240 m long passes a pole in 24 seconds. How long will it take to pass a platform 650 m long?
(a) 65 seconds
(b) 89 seconds
(c) 95 seconds
(d) 100 seconds
Correct Answer: (b) 89 seconds [Explanation: Speed of train = Length of train / Time = 240 m / 24 s = 10 m/s. Time to cross platform = (Length of train + Length of platform) / Speed = (240 + 650) / 10 = 890 / 10 = 89 seconds.]

* Q2. A man can row downstream at 14 km/h and upstream at 8 km/h. What is the speed of the stream?
(a) 2 km/h
(b) 3 km/h
(c) 4 km/h
(d) 6 km/h
Correct Answer: (b) 3 km/h [Explanation: Speed of stream v = (Downstream - Upstream) / 2 = (14 - 8) / 2 = 6 / 2 = 3 km/h.]

* Q3. Pipe A can fill a tank in 20 hours and Pipe B can fill it in 30 hours. Both pipes are opened together, but Pipe A is closed 8 hours before the tank is full. What is the total time taken to fill the tank?
(a) 15 hours
(b) 16.8 hours
(c) 18 hours
(d) 20 hours
Correct Answer: (b) 16.8 hours [Explanation: Total capacity = LCM(20, 30) = 60 units. Rate of A = 3 units/hr; Rate of B = 2 units/hr. In the last 8 hours, only B works, doing 8 * 2 = 16 units. Remaining work = 60 - 16 = 44 units was done by A and B together at rate (3+2)=5 units/hr. Time together = 44 / 5 = 8.8 hours. Total time = 8.8 + 8 = 16.8 hours.]

* Q4. A car travels from town A to town B at 60 km/h and returns from town B to town A along the same route at 40 km/h. What is the average speed of the car for the entire journey?
(a) 48 km/h
(b) 50 km/h
(c) 52 km/h
(d) 54 km/h
Correct Answer: (a) 48 km/h [Explanation: Average speed for equal distance = 2xy / (x + y) = (2 * 60 * 40) / (60 + 40) = 4800 / 100 = 48 km/h.]

* Q5. If 8 men or 12 women can reap a harvest in 25 days, in how many days can 6 men and 11 women reap the same harvest?
(a) 12 days
(b) 15 days
(c) 18 days
(d) 20 days
Correct Answer: (b) 15 days [Explanation: 8 Men = 12 Women => 1 Man = 1.5 Women. 6 Men + 11 Women = (6 * 1.5) + 11 = 9 + 11 = 20 Women. Using M1 * D1 = M2 * D2: 12 Women * 25 Days = 20 Women * D2 => D2 = (12 * 25) / 20 = 300 / 20 = 15 days.]
